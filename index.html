Hello Love,

It has been two years already. Happy Birthday to one of the sweetest person that I will always love. Today is your special day, not because imuha ning birthday but this is the day the world has been blessed by the most caring, loving, beautiful, and strong girl and I am always honored to be loved by you. You gave meaning to my world and to those others that you love. You may not be perfect in others' eye but you will always be in my heart.

Things have been rough sa atoa especially sa Acads and hapit nata graduating but I always know that you will always fight no matter what to achieve the goals that you always wanted. Kabalo jud ku kay grabe jud kay ka perfectionist dai. Makaingon jud ku na mas perfectionist pa kay ka kaysa akoa. Thank you pud lab sa time na we spent together na halos everyday jud and I will forever cherish and not be tired from it because you made my day always. Thank you lab sa pag buy ug mga gifts sa akoa ug panglibre sa mga food. I appreciate you buying these gifts to the point gina safe keep najud nako kay mahadlok kug mawala unya gaka suko najud ka nako kay di nako gina gamit (sorry lab hehe). It really doesnt matter what kind of gift na imung gina hatag sa akoa lab as long as ganahan jud kag hatag sa akoa ug mga inana and as long as you're there.

One thing I also love about you lab kay is being affectionate, since Day 1 kiligun jud ko anytime naay accidental touches hantud nag holding2 hands hantud nag sgee nag pa bebe sa akoa hehehe. It always warms my heart na you feel safe around me especially kadum2 pako na grabe pa imung tulong sa Weecup. I still remember how I am excited spending time with you on Xavier Days tung last two years. Up until now I am still excited to be with you every single day knowing na makita najud nako ang akung pinangga kaayo na bebi. I still remember the time na nag congratulate ka sa koa tung na deans list unya kato pamn tung engg week, gwapa kay ka ato nahan kay kus imung makeup ato. It really touched my heart na first time jud ku kadungong na naay nag congratulate sa akoa in many years.

I also love the way ka mangluod sa akoa. Usahay kay kiligun ko usahay pud mahimuot sad. Gaka kyutan jud ko permi inig mag inana jud kas ako. I also love the way you on how you encourage and comfort others especially with your friends. On how you always do more sa ilaha ug sa akoa. I know gaka disappoint lng ka kay dle siya gaka appreciate sa uban pero I always see you and I am proud. I always love hearing your favorite sitcoms, and watching your favorite shows with you. I always love hearing your hobbies how you always wanted to buy that camera or how you always share your political beliefs or how you always dance when you're happy or how you always play roblox before and tetris with me or how you build gundam with me or go on a shopping spree sa online or sa ukay2 sad or mag sge tag gasto kay sgee tag pamalit ug lami na pagkaon.

From Ms Picebreaker to Physics graduate to Ms Photographer to First Lady to Ms Events Head and to Struct and Thesis Fighter. I am always proud of you on how much you've grown and no amount can measure. I will be always be there for you no matter what.

Medyo taas2 na ako message lab hehe. I will always pray for you and your family. May God always bless your kind heart and may you always flourish and the find the strength through your journey. Please dont ever forget how much special you are even when you dont feel it.

Enjoy your day my love. You deserve the very best. Happy 22nd Birthday lablab and I love you ara. :w
